Who Am I

The Story Behind the Work

I'm an Automation Engineer and Global PGET at Volvo Group, passionate about turning complex, manual workflows into simpler, smarter, and more connected digital solutions.

My work sits at the intersection of automation, data, and AI — digitalizing manual processes, centralizing scattered information, and creating structured, reliable data foundations that can eventually power intelligent solutions. For me, automation isn't just about replacing manual work; it's about making processes more efficient, transparent, scalable, and easier for people to work with.

A defining part of my Global PGET journey has been the opportunity to work across Sweden, France, and the USA, collaborating with teams from different functions, backgrounds, and ways of working.

Spending four months working with the France team in Lyon was particularly meaningful. It gave me the opportunity to step beyond my usual environment, work closely with an international team, understand processes from a different perspective, and experience what it truly means to build solutions across countries and cultures. It also taught me that successful digitalization isn't only about technology — it's about understanding the people, processes, and problems behind it.

As part of this journey, I've contributed to initiatives around circularity, sustainability, automation, and data-driven transformation — exploring how global manufacturing operations can reduce waste, extend product lifecycles, and build more responsible and future-ready ways of working.

Looking back, my journey has been about more than building tools or automating processes. It has been about learning how technology can connect people, processes, and data across borders — and using that connection to create meaningful change.
